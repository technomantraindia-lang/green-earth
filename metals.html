Non Ferrous & Ferrous Metals

At Green Earth Commodity FZ LLC, we are key players in the international trading of Ferrous and Non-Ferrous metal scrap. We act as a vital link between major metal recyclers/collectors in source markets and industrial consumers, including steel mills, foundries, and smelting operations worldwide.

Our sourcing is focused on premium grades, ensuring that our clients receive metal scrap that meets strict international specifications (such as ISRI standards). We manage the complete supply chain, guaranteeing that grade verification, weight assessment, and customs logistics are executed with absolute transparency and efficiency.

Our Product Portfolio Includes

Ferrous Metal Scrap

High-melting scrap grades for steel manufacturing and iron foundries:

  • HMS 1 & 2 (Heavy Melting Steel): Sourced in standard 80:20 ratios, compliant with ISRI 200-206 specifications.
  • Shredded Scrap: Compliant with ISRI 211 grade, high density and clean metallic composition.
  • Plate & Structural Scrap: Clean cut pieces, minimal impurities, ideal for foundry induction furnaces.
  • Bonus Grade Scrap: Premium heavy iron/steel plates, rails, and structural steel scrap.

Non-Ferrous Metal Scrap

Recyclable non-ferrous metal grades for secondary smelters and metal refiners:

  • Aluminium Scrap: Tense (mixed castings), Tabor (sheet aluminium), Extrusions (6063), and clean alloy wheels.
  • Stainless Steel Scrap: Mainly Grade 304 (18-8 solids) and Grade 316 solids/turnings, sorted and verified.
  • Copper Scrap: Berry/Candy (No.1 copper wire), Birch/Cliff (No.2 copper), and copper cathodes.
  • Brass Scrap: Honey grade yellow brass scrap, ocean brass, and rod brass turnings.
