Ferrous Metal Scrap

Our Ferrous Metal Scrap portfolio includes:

  • HMS 1: Heavy Melting Steel Scrap consisting of clean, thick steel sections with minimal contamination, suitable for Steel Mills and Foundries.
  • HMS 1 & 2 (80:20): Standard Blend of Heavy Melting Steel Scrap in an 80:20 ratio, compliant with ISRI Specifications and widely used for Steel Production.
  • Shredded Steel Scrap: Uniformly Shredded Ferrous Scrap with high density, low impurities, and excellent Melting Efficiency.
  • Busheling Scrap: Premium Clean New-Production Steel Offcuts and Stampings, free from coatings and contaminants.
  • Plate & Structural (P&S) Scrap: Heavy Structural Steel, Beams, Channels, and Plate Scrap with minimal impurities, ideal for Electric Arc Furnaces and Foundries.
  • Used Rail Scrap: Used Railway Rails processed to Customer Specifications for Re-Rolling or Melting Applications.
  • Cast Iron Scrap: Clean Cast Iron Components including Engine Blocks, Machine Parts, and Industrial Castings for Foundry Use.
  • Steel Turnings: Clean Machining Turnings and Borings with controlled moisture and low contamination for Steel Recycling.
  • Steel Punching Scrap: High-Quality Punched Steel Offcuts generated during Manufacturing, offering excellent Furnace Yield.
  • Steel Offcuts & End Cuts: Clean Steel Remnants from Fabrication and Processing, suitable for Remelting and Recycling.
  • Moon Cut / Windmill Plates: Heavy-Duty Steel Plates sourced from Decommissioned Wind Turbines and Industrial Structures, processed into Moon-Cut sizes for efficient Handling and Furnace charging. Their High-Density Steel content and Excellent Metal recovery make them ideal for Steel Mills, Foundries, and Recycling Operations.
  • Alloy Steel Scrap: Recyclable Alloy Steel containing elements such as Chromium, Nickel, Molybdenum, or Vanadium for Specialty Steel Production.

Non-Ferrous Metal Scrap

Our Non-Ferrous Metal Scrap portfolio includes:

  • Aluminium Scrap: Clean Recycled Aluminium sourced from Industrial, Automotive, and Manufacturing Sectors, suitable for Remelting and Secondary Aluminium Production.
  • Aluminium Wheel Scrap: Clean Used and Rejected Aluminium Alloy Wheels with high Metal Recovery for Foundries and Alloy Manufacturers.
  • Aluminium UBC Scrap: Used Beverage Cans processed to Industry Standards with low contamination for Aluminium Recycling.
  • Aluminium Tense Scrap: Mixed Cast Aluminium Scrap consisting of clean Engine Parts, Housings, and Automotive Castings.
  • Aluminium Extrusion Scrap: Clean Aluminium Extrusion Profiles from Construction and Industrial Applications, available Painted or Unpainted.
  • Stainless Steel Scrap: High-Quality Recyclable Stainless-Steel Scrap sourced from Industrial, Manufacturing, and Demolition Sectors, suitable for Stainless Steel Production and Alloy Recovery.
  • Lead Scrap: Recyclable Lead Materials including Sheets, Pipes, Cable Sheathing, and Industrial Lead Products.
  • Lead Battery Scrap: Used Lead-Acid Batteries processed for Lead Recovery and Recycling.
  • Zinc Scrap: Clean Zinc Die-Cast, Sheet, and Galvanizing Residues for Secondary Zinc Production.
  • Electric Motor Scrap: Used Electric Motors containing recoverable Copper, Aluminium, and Steel Components.
  • Mixed Electric Motor Scrap: Mixed-Grade Electric Motors from Industrial, Automotive, and Household Equipment, suitable for Copper, Aluminium, and Steel Recovery.
  • Refrigerator Compressor Scrap: Used Refrigerator and Air-Conditioner Compressors containing recoverable Copper, Steel, and Aluminium, processed for efficient Metal Recycling.
