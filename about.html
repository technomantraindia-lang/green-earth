About Us –
Green Earth Commodity FZ LLC

Green Earth Commodity FZ LLC is an international trading company specializing in the sourcing, marketing, and supply of recycled Metals, Rubber, Paper, and Plastic commodities.

Led by a management team with over two decades of expertise in the Global Recycling and Commodity Trading industry, we deliver reliable, competitive, and sustainable trading solutions to customers across international markets.

Our extensive industry expertise, combined with long-standing relationships with trusted suppliers and customers, enables us to provide consistent quality, dependable sourcing, and efficient supply chain solutions. With a strong network across Europe, United Kingdom, United States, Middle East, and Asia, we are well positioned to meet the diverse requirements of our global partners.

At Green Earth Commodity FZ LLC, we are committed to building long-term partnerships founded on Integrity, Transparency, and Professionalism. By combining market expertise with operational excellence, competitive pricing, and efficient logistics, we create lasting value for our customers while promoting responsible and sustainable trade.

As a company focused on the recycling industry, we actively support the Global Circular Economy by giving recyclable materials a second life and helping reduce landfill waste, conserve natural resources, and lower carbon emissions. Through responsible sourcing and international trade, we contribute to transforming waste into valuable raw materials, supporting manufacturers and industries in building a more sustainable and resource-efficient future.

Our mission is to be a trusted global trading partner, connecting suppliers and buyers through ethical business practices, reliable execution, and a shared commitment to environmental sustainability.
