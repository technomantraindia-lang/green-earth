Non-Prime & Secondary Steel

Our Non-Prime & Secondary Steel portfolio includes:

  • Secondary Hot Rolled Coils (HRC): Commercial-Grade Hot Rolled Coils with minor surface imperfections, suitable for Structural and General Fabrication Applications.
  • Secondary Cold Rolled Coils (CRC): Cold Rolled Coils with cosmetic defects while maintaining excellent Mechanical Properties for Industrial Use.
  • Secondary Galvanized Steel Coils (GI): Zinc-Coated Steel Coils with minor visual defects, ideal for Roofing, Fabrication, and Manufacturing.
  • Secondary Galvalume Steel Coils (GL): Aluminium-Zinc Coated Steel Coils offering excellent Corrosion Resistance with acceptable surface variations.
  • Secondary PPGI Coils: Pre-Painted Galvanized Steel Coils with minor coating or appearance imperfections for Cost-Effective Applications.
  • Secondary Tinplate Coils & Sheets: Tin-Coated Steel Products with minor cosmetic defects, suitable for Industrial and Packaging Applications.
  • End Cuts & Offcuts: Steel Remnants and Cut-to-Length Materials generated during Processing, suitable for Fabrication and Manufacturing.
  • Electrical Steel (CRGO & CRNGO): Secondary and Non-Prime Grain-Oriented and Non-Grain-Oriented Electrical Steel for Transformer and Motor Applications.
  • Tin Free Steel (TFS/ECCS): Secondary Tin-Free Steel Products suitable for Packaging and Industrial Manufacturing.
  • Mixed Steel Sheets (HR/CR/HDG/EG/PPGI): Mixed Lots of Hot Rolled, Cold Rolled, Hot-Dip Galvanized, Electro-Galvanized, and Pre-Painted Galvanized Steel Sheets available in Secondary and Non-Prime Quality.
  • Wire Rods: Secondary and Non-Prime Wire Rods suitable for Wire Drawing, Fasteners, Welding Electrodes, and General Engineering Applications.
  • Seamless Pipes: Secondary and Non-Prime Seamless Steel Pipes with minor cosmetic imperfections, suitable for Structural, Mechanical, and Industrial Applications.
